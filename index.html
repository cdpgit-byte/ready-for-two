AI for successful teams. In the US.

Your Business page already makes the case. This is how I'd carry it across the Atlantic.

  • Keep your headline: independent of any single AI provider

    US teams are arguing right now about which provider to standardize on. Mono lets them skip the argument and use the leaders today and tomorrow.

  • Start with teams that live in your six use cases

    Researching customers and comparing quotes is my working week as an account manager. Agencies, consultancies and sales teams of 5 to 200 do the same, and a per-user price is easy for them to approve.

  • Rewrite the trust story for an American buyer

    No training on their data, anonymised customer names, and a signed contract with a Data Processing Agreement. A US company with European clients will care that data sits in Germany. The others need to hear the first three.

  • Answer "Get in touch" during the US day

    That contract request is the start of a sales conversation. A buyer in Seattle or New York who writes at 10:00 should hear back before lunch, not the next morning.
